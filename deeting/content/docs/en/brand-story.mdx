title: Why Deeting Is Named Diting

## The name

“Diting” comes from a mythical creature in Chinese tradition, often associated with listening, discernment, insight, and guardianship.

The name was chosen because that symbolism maps closely to what the product is trying to become:

- something that senses context
- remembers important information
- evolves across longer workflows

## Brand positioning

The core positioning of Deeting is:

Intelligent Context & Memory Hub

This means the product is not just a chat interface and not just a container for tools.  
It is intended to act as a hub connecting conversation, memory, knowledge, tools, and execution flow.

## Visual language

The visual centerpiece of Deeting is a cyber-mythical beast inspired by Diting.

Against a deep black background, gold and dark-purple light paths form the silhouette of a horned mythical creature. The result combines:

- cultural depth from mythic symbolism
- modern technical energy from a cybernetic visual system

The goal is not nostalgia. The goal is a future-facing identity with memory, perception, and direction.

## Brand line

Listen. Remember. Evolve.

These three words also act as a product metaphor:

- `Listen`
  understand input, context, and change

- `Remember`
  retain memory, knowledge, and continuity

- `Evolve`
  improve through execution, feedback, and accumulation

## Why this matters

Many products are good at answering a single question.  
Fewer are designed to stay with the user across a longer process.

That is the ambition behind Deeting:

- not just respond once
- not just call a tool once
- but connect people, context, capability, and execution into a more coherent system
